## Cross-vendor read (Codex gpt-6.1-sol, high; Agencil seats failed to launch)

Binding defect: feedback without a progress requirement; a new message is new work even when it
repeats a resolved question or asks nothing. A team-wide dollar increase is not demonstrated;
collect teammates' job exports, builds and flows over matched windows before claiming it.

Rules that keep speed: a mention is a notification, not a dispatch; an ask names an unresolved
choice or a requested fact or action for the recipient, with a stable decision id and revision;
status, acknowledgements, reminders and copied mentions never dispatch; decide at once on a new
revision, deduplicate across Macs by repo, issue, decision id and revision, never by comment id or
wording; one ruling plus one scoped confirmation per decision, then a human once; persist the
outcome (decided, needs a human fact, no question, failed) and post nothing when there is no
question; a ruling is decision id, choice, reason, evidence, next step, at most 150 words; one
canonical plan in the repo, comments carry decisions and changed findings only, under 200 words,
enforced in the outcome handling, not by prompt adjectives.

On the proposals: excluding stale notices is right, "Bton wrote it" is too broad (a triager
comment can carry a real question); routing agent handoffs to a human blocks the intended fast
agent-to-agent decisions, keep them within delegated authority and send humans only private facts
and risk exceptions; a blanket Pause is containment, not a fix.
